Slide 6 — New TrailBlaze Prompt (read aloud, do not distribute) Mid-cycle context — same Session 1 gate, no new reveals

> The CEO called an all-hands yesterday. She opened with: "I've already talked to the board. We're building a mobile app. We need to show progress by Q3." > > The Head of Operations said nothing in the meeting. Afterwards they told two team members: "The app isn't the problem. We've had three guides quit in the last two months and nobody upstairs knows it." > > The Marketing Lead sent an email after the meeting: "I support the app directionally, but we need to be clear — the booking drop is a discovery and trust problem, not a tech problem." > > The BA has not been asked for a recommendation yet. The CEO mentioned the BA's involvement at the all-hands but did not clarify what the BA's role is.

> Facilitator note: Do not distribute this as a handout. Read it aloud or show it on screen. The group works from memory and notes — just like a real brief.

Slide 7 — Live BACCM Workshop (30 min) Groups of 3–4. Same groups as Session 1A if possible.

The task: Using only the BACCM — apply each element to the new prompt. The SME is in the room and will push back.

BACCM ElementQuestion to workCommon trap
ChangeWhat transformation is being requested — and by whom?Accepting the CEO's framing without challenge
NeedWhat is actually broken? What evidence do you have?Conflating a symptom with a root cause
SolutionCan you fill this in yet? Why or why not?Filling it in prematurely because the CEO already announced one
StakeholderWho has standing in this change — including those not in the room?Missing the Head of Operations, Lead Guide, the guides who quit
ValueWhat does success look like, and who defines it?Measuring only what the CEO can see
ContextWhat is forcing this conversation right now?Ignoring board pressure and guide attrition as separate context inputs

SME participates as themselves: Comments on approach, asks probing questions, challenges where real-world experience differs from what the group assumes.

Key facilitator interrupt (use if group fills in SOLUTION too quickly): > "The CEO has already announced the solution. Does that mean the BA's SOLUTION box is filled? What is the BA's job here?"

Slide 8 — Share-Out + SME Response (built into the 30 min block)

  • Each group gives 60 seconds on their NEED and VALUE entries — those are the two most contested
  • SME responds to each: "On a real engagement, here's how that plays out..."
  • Facilitator listens for: groups who defaulted to the CEO's framing, groups who surfaced the guide attrition signal

Facilitator redirect if groups converge too quickly: > "Everyone agreed on the same NEED. Is that reassuring or is it a warning sign?"

Slide 9 — Debrief: What the Model Does Under Pressure (10 min) Questions for the group — SME answers alongside:

  1. The solution was announced before the BA was engaged. What is the BA's move?
  • Not to stop the app — to ensure the NEED is defined before the app is scoped
  • BACCM gives you a professional frame for that conversation
  1. The Head of Operations said nothing in the meeting. Is that person a stakeholder? What kind?
  • High influence, currently disengaged — the most dangerous quadrant
  • Guide attrition is a context signal that changes the BACCM, not a separate problem
  1. How do you document competing stakeholder diagnoses without picking a side?
  • NEED stays open until elicitation confirms it
  • The BACCM does not demand consensus — it demands traceability

> Open Q&A: 5 minutes — direct questions to the SME. Real work only.

Slide 10 — What the BACCM Is Actually For

  • The BACCM is your route map — not the summit flag
  • When a solution is announced before a need is defined, the BACCM is how a BA asks: "How do we know that route leads to the right summit?"
  • When stakeholders disagree, the BACCM shows you where the disagreement lives — usually in NEED or VALUE, not in SOLUTION
  • When the terrain is noisy, the BACCM separates what is driving the change from what is being changed
  • Exam framing: ECBA questions about BACCM often test whether you can identify the correct element — especially when the scenario is designed to push you toward SOLUTION before NEED is confirmed

Slide 11 — Stretch Question + Homework Bridge (5 min) > [SME Name] asks the group: > "How would you use the BACCM to push back on a solution that leadership has already decided on — without losing the room?"

This is not an additional homework assignment. Bring your answer to the Session 2 share-out.

The facilitator adds context for Session 2: > "TrailBlaze has now hired a BA. Leadership interviews are scheduled. But the CEO has already committed to the board. Your stretch question is the first thing you'll face when you walk into that building."
